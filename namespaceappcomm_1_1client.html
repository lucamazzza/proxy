enum class appcomm::client::ConnectionState
strong

Enumeration of possible connection states.

Represents the lifecycle of the client's connection to the communication backend. Used to manage UI state, reconnection logic, and network behavior.

Enumerator
Disconnected 

No active connection.

Disconnecting 

Connection is being closed.

Connected 

Connection is established and active.

Connecting 

Connection attempt in progress.

Reconnecting 

Attempting to restore a lost connection.

Failed 

Connection attempt failed.
