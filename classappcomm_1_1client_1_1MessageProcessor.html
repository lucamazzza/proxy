Detailed Description

Processes incoming messages and ensures their validity and consistency.

The MessageProcessor is responsible for handling messages received from the backend. It performs validation, detects duplicates, and checks for gaps in the message sequence based on the current client state.

It does not own the ClientState, but operates on it to maintain consistency between received messages and the client’s internal state.
